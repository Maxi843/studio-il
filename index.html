Studio Il

Practice: Architecture, Interiors, Documentation

Architects: Hariesh RG, Pavethran RG

Location: Erode, Tamil Nadu

Studio Il is an architecture and interior design practice in Erode, Tamil Nadu. Il, இல், is the Tamil word for home, and it is where every project begins: with how people live, work and gather in a place.

We design the building and its rooms together, with structure, light, joinery and furniture drawn as one idea, and show every space in full before it is built.
